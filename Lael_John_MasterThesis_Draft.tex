\chapter*{Foreword}

This thesis aims to document the mathematical progress made around Comon's Conjecture (a question about tensor rank and symmetry). The thesis covers some attempts made to prove the conjecture in various  contexts since its statement in 2008. The main focus of this work centers around the methodology proposed by Shitov (both published \cite{shitov_counterexample_2018} and preprints \cites{shitov_comons_2020,shitov_higher_2024,shitov_higher_2024-1}) that aims to construct a valid counterexample to the conjecture. The culmination of this thesis is an examination of Wang and Seigal's paper \cite{wang_lower_2023} detailing the construction of a valid counterexample in $\reals$. While the existence of a counterexample in $\complex$ is still an open question, this thesis concludes that the ideas detailed within these papers provide a valid pathway to one.
